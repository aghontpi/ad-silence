<b>Features:</b>

- <b>Custom Apps Support</b>: Configure and silence ads in ANY app.
- Pre-configured for Accuradio, Spotify (Full & Lite), Tidal, Soundcloud, Pandora & LiveOne.
- Lightweight (~ 150KB)
- Minimal UI
- <b>Debug Log View</b>: Integrated logging for easy troubleshooting.
- No bloat/external dependencies
- All locally processed, no data collection
- No inapp purchases or ads
- Open source

<b>If you want to know on how this works & more.. check github.</b>
