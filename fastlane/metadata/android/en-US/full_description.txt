<b>Features:</b>

- Mute ads on <b>Accuradio</b>, <b>Spotify</b> (Full & Lite), <b>Pandora</b>, <b>Soundcloud</b> or <b>Any app</b> with Custom App Option
- <b>Casting Support</b>: Supports casting (Google Chromecast, etc)
- <b>Fully Configurable</b>: Edit preloaded apps, add new custom apps, and adjust keywords or unmute delays for precise control.
- <b>Debug Log View</b>: Integrated logging for easy troubleshooting.
- Minimal UI & Lightweight (~ 150KB)
- No bloat/external dependencies
- All locally processed, no data collection
- No inapp purchases or ads
- Open source

<b>If you want to know on how this works & more.. check github.</b>
